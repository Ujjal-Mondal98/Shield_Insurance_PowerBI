# 🛡️ Shield Insurance Analytics Dashboard | Business Analytics Case Study (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Virtual Internship at Codebasics**, where I worked on a simulated real-world business analytics case study for **Shield Insurance**.

The objective was not only to build dashboards, but to create a **business-focused analytical solution** capable of helping stakeholders monitor performance, understand customer behavior, and support strategic decision-making.

The final output is an **interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard**, designed with a strong focus on:

✔ Business insights  
✔ Analytical storytelling  
✔ User experience (UX) & dashboard design  
✔ Decision-support analytics  

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer key business questions:

- How is overall business performance changing over time?
- Which sales channels contribute most to revenue and customer acquisition?
- Which customer age groups drive growth?
- How do customer demographics influence policy preferences and settlement expectations?
- What patterns can support future strategic decisions?

---

# 🧱 Report Structure

The report consists of the following analytical sections:

🏠 **Home Page** → Navigation & Business Context  
📊 **General View** → Overall KPI Performance  
🧭 **Sales Mode Analysis** → Channel Performance Analysis  
👥 **Age Group Analysis** → Demographic & Customer Behaviour Analysis

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Customer growth and revenue growth move closely together, indicating that customer acquisition strongly influences business performance.
- Revenue contribution is uneven, with a few cities contributing a major share.
- The **31–40 age group** emerges as one of the strongest contributors to revenue generation.
- Monthly spikes suggest seasonal patterns or campaign-driven customer activity.

---

## 💡 Business Meaning

This page establishes **baseline business performance**, helping stakeholders understand overall growth and identify key contributing customer segments.
